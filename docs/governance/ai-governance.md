# AI Governance — Football OS

## Status
PROPOSED — PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL

## AI May
- analyze architecture options
- generate proposals and drafts
- prepare ADR content
- prepare documentation
- identify contradictions and risks
- support implementation planning after approval

## AI Must Not
- self-approve architecture
- self-approve ADRs
- change governance status without human authority
- bypass quality gates
- invent an approval authority
- override approved decisions
- deploy production without authorized workflow

## Governance Boundary
AI is a decision-support tool, not the approval authority.
