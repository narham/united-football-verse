# Safeguarding Model — Football OS

## Principle
Child's Interest Prevails. No actor receives more information than required for a legitimate role.

## Minor Detection
A person is treated as a protected minor when age is below the jurisdictional threshold for adulthood, or when an organization marks them as a minor for safeguarding purposes.

## Guardian Relationship
Guardian access is granted through explicit relationship and purpose-bound consent, not through general organization membership.

## Consent Model
- Consent is purpose-specific
- Consent can be withdrawn
- Consent expiry and audit are required

## Restricted Data
The following data require restricted access:
- date of birth
- photo
- guardian contact details
- address
- identity documents
- medical-related data
- assessment data

## Access Controls
- Coaches and staff may access only the minimum data necessary for training, welfare, and operational duties.
- Guardians receive role-limited access to their minor's schedule, attendance, and approved profile data.
- Professional access to safeguarded data must be logged and audited.

## Retention and Deletion
- Retention must follow policy and legal obligations.
- Deletion and export must be capability-based and auditable.
